# Launch checklist: filings, contracts, insurance

*Draft 2, October 30, 2026. Tick these off in order. Items marked ⚖️ need the aviation attorney, 💲 the CPA.*

## A. Company and ownership
- [ ] Partner term sheet with Chief Pilot: role, salary, 40% of Class B promote vesting over 4 years (1-year cliff), buy-sell ⚖️
- [ ] AircraftCo #1 LLC formed under Fund I (Colorado, or Delaware registered in Colorado) ⚖️
- [ ] Operating agreements: reserved decisions, capital calls, transfer restrictions ⚖️
- [ ] EINs, bank accounts, accounting system (QuickBooks or similar), bookkeeping by aircraft
- [ ] Key-person life and disability insurance on Chief Pilot
- [ ] Trademark / name search for the brand

## A2. Investor raise (securities counsel)
- [ ] Securities counsel engaged; Reg D 506(b) or 506(c) chosen
- [ ] Sponsor LLC and Fund I LLC formed; Class A / Class B unit terms (`09-Investor-Structure-GP-LP.md`)
- [ ] Private Placement Memorandum, operating agreement, subscription agreement, accredited-investor questionnaire
- [ ] Anchor investors soft-circled; guarantor decision made with the lender
- [ ] Escrow for subscriptions; Form D filed within 15 days of first sale; Colorado and other state notice filings
- [ ] Investor reporting set up: quarterly reports, annual K-1s

## B. Tax 💲
- [ ] Written CPA memo: bonus depreciation, material participation, passive and at-risk limits
- [ ] State sales/use tax plan for the purchase (delivery location, Colorado exemptions)
- [ ] Business-use records kept from day one (100% charter, no personal use)
- [ ] Federal excise tax (7.5%) handled by the operator: confirm in the management agreement who collects and remits
- [ ] Colorado and county business personal property tax filings for the aircraft

## C. Financing
- [ ] Personal financial statement, 3 years of tax returns, liquidity verification
- [ ] Term sheets from 2-3 aviation lenders (LTV, rate, amortization, balloon, prepayment, covenants)
- [ ] Lender requirements: insurance (lender loss payee), reserve accounts, charter use consent
- [ ] Loan documents, security agreement, International Registry filings ⚖️

## D. Aircraft purchase
- [ ] Buy-side broker agreement
- [ ] LOI with refundable deposit into escrow ⚖️
- [ ] Pre-buy at a Textron service center: records, damage history, engine and APU program status, borescope
- [ ] Aircraft purchase agreement; technical acceptance; bill of sale ⚖️
- [ ] FAA registration (AC Form 8050-1), U.S. citizenship confirmation, International Registry, escrow closing
- [ ] Engine ESP and APU program transfer confirmed in writing
- [ ] CAMP / maintenance tracking transferred

## E. Management company and operations
- [ ] RFP issued and scored (`06-Management-Company-RFP.md`)
- [ ] Written evidence: Part 135 certificate, ARGUS/Wyvern ratings and audit dates, IS-BAO if any, **NetJets/fractional supplier status**
- [ ] **Committed hours plan (750+ billable/yr)** or minimum revenue guarantee
- [ ] Management agreement reviewed ⚖️: fees, commission, cost pass-through, owner audit rights, crew employment, termination, monthly reporting, approval limits for maintenance spend
- [ ] Aircraft lease or charter arrangement between AircraftCo and the operator (operational control rests with the certificate holder) ⚖️
- [ ] Aircraft added to the operator's OpSpecs; conformity inspection complete
- [ ] Hangar lease at KBJC; fuel contract or card
- [ ] Wi-Fi, cabin standard and catering standard meet fractional requirements
- [ ] Crew hired; Part 135 initial and recurrent training scheduled; drug and alcohol testing enrollment (operator)

## F. Insurance
- [ ] Hull all-risk at agreed value (lender as loss payee)
- [ ] Liability $100M+ combined single limit (check what NetJets/fractionals require; may be higher)
- [ ] Hull war and allied perils
- [ ] AircraftCo named insured or additional insured on the operator's policy, with waiver of subrogation
- [ ] Non-owned aircraft liability for HoldCo
- [ ] Directors and officers / general liability for HoldCo

## G. Own Part 135 certificate (Phase 2)
- [ ] OpCo formed; required management (Director of Operations, Chief Pilot, Director of Maintenance) hired with FAA-qualifying experience
- [ ] FAA pre-application statement of intent; formal application; manuals; training program; SMS
- [ ] Drug and alcohol testing program
- [ ] DOT air taxi registration (14 CFR Part 298)
- [ ] TSA security program
- [ ] IRS excise tax registration and quarterly Form 720 filing
- [ ] ARGUS and Wyvern applications; fractional supplier audits in OpCo's name
