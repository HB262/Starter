# One-page summary: Front Range Jet Partners (working name)

*Draft 4, October 30, 2026. Updated with comments from Derek, the intended Chief Pilot. Figures come from `financials/Jet_Business_Financial_Model.xlsx` (base scenario unless noted). They are estimates to be replaced with real quotes.*

## The idea
Buy a lightly used **Cessna Citation Sovereign+** (about 2015), base it at **Rocky Mountain Metropolitan Airport (KBJC), Broomfield, CO**, and place it on **Executive Jet Management's (EJM) Part 135 certificate**. EJM is NetJets' affiliated management company. Its managed fleet flies about **95% NetJets customers**, so the jet earns its hours as NetJets overflow without us selling charter. There is no personal use.

**Henry** is the founder and general partner. **Derek**, a type-rated PIC who flies today for an operation on EJM's certificate, joins as **Chief Pilot partner** and runs day-to-day operations. **Outside investors fund the equity** through a GP/LP-style LLC; Henry and Derek earn a promote.

## Why it can work
- **EJM does the heavy lifting:** certificate, OpSpecs, negotiated fuel and services pricing, hangars, A&P mechanics, pilots, admin, and NetJets compliance for aircraft and crews. Both pilots hold full PIC type ratings and attend FlightSafety recurrent every 6 months at EJM's rates.
- **NetJets feeds the work:** with EJM routing NetJets trips, the jet doesn't have to hunt for charter.
- **A live demand driver:** NetJets now requires at least **5,500 ft of runway in the rain for its Phenoms**, after runway excursions. Derek's crews are covering many of those Phenom owners. The Sovereign+ needs about 3,530 ft to take off.
- 100% first-year bonus depreciation gives investors about **$2.70 of deductions per $1 invested** in year one.

## Investor structure
- Fund I LLC: investors hold Class A units; the sponsor holds Class B units, split **60% Henry / 40% Derek** (his share vests over 4 years). Henry is the general partner and takes no salary at the start.
- Distributions: return of capital, then an **8% preferred return**, then **80/20** split. 1% acquisition fee; Reg D private placement.
- Lenders may cap an investor-owned jet at 60-70% LTV unless an anchor investor guarantees the loan.

## Gates and stop-loss
1. **Gate 1 (Feb 2027):** EJM gives, in writing, its NetJets rate card for a Sovereign+, a projected-hours letter, and its fee and commission, and these clear debt service in the model.
2. **Gate 2 (Mar 2027):** lender term sheet, plus anchor investors soft-circled for about $4.2M.
3. **Gate 3 (May 2027):** pre-buy, close and onboard to EJM. **Revenue flying July 2027.**
4. **Stop-loss:** if trailing-12 cash after debt is worse than **($750K)** by mid-2029, sell Jet #1 and buy no more jets.
5. **2029:** decide on our own certificate only if NetJets access survives the move. **2034-36:** sell to a consolidator.
